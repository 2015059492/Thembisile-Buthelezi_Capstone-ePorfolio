About me

From the genetics lab to the digital classroom

I'm Thembisile Buthelezi, a Master of Education candidate in Educational Technology at the University of Delaware. My path began with a BSc in Genetics and Microbiology, and grew into teaching through a PGCE (cum laude) and a BEd Honours in Science Education (cum laude).

Along the way I taught Life Sciences and Mathematical Literacy, supported remote schools with mathematics and science, and tutored pre-service Foundation Phase teachers in coding and robotics with Scratch Junior and OctoStudio.

I believe technology should widen access, not narrow it. My work focuses on designing blended, collaborative learning experiences grounded in research and in the real needs of learners and educators.
